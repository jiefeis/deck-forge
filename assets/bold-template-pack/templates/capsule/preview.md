## Visual Snapshot

Cover recipe:

- Surface: cream #F5F5F0 with one to three soft radial accent glows at 6–15% opacity, under the always-on 4% fractal-grain multiply overlay; slide padding 48px top/bottom, 64px sides.
- Title pill above the headline: yellow #F2D160 fill, 2px #1E1E1E outline, 24px/56px padding, Space Grotesk 14px uppercase at 0.12em tracking.
- Title: Bodoni Moda weight 800 at 154px, line-height 0.9, -0.02em tracking, sentence case, always ink #1A1A1A — serif headlines are never candy-colored, never uppercase.
- Subtitle: Space Grotesk 29px uppercase at 0.15em tracking, ink at 0.6–0.7 opacity, below the title.
- Accent rule: a 60×4 coral #E85D4E pill sitting under the subtitle.
- Wallpaper: 5–8 decorative candy pills (60–160px wide, 35–90px tall) tilted -20° to +25° around the edges, each holding one uppercase Space Grotesk word at 11px with the 2px outline and no shadow — bare corners read as broken.
- Chrome: nav-dot column at the right edge and a slide counter at bottom-right; any page number that must survive PDF export is a slide-internal element.
