## Visual Snapshot

Cover recipe:

- Surface: full-bleed dark forest green #232E26 with the wood-brown radial glow anchored bottom-right (55% wide × 70% tall, rgba(122,78,36,0.28) → transparent); cover slides suppress both the chrome band and the foot band.
- Padding: 106px left/right, 59px top/bottom — content sits in the middle ~89% of the stage.
- Kicker: DM Mono uppercase 19px, 0.12em tracking, warm orange #C07030, above the title.
- Rule: the 32px × 1px warm-orange accent line under the kicker.
- Title: Bricolage Grotesque weight 800 at 323px, mixed case (never uppercase), line-height 0.88, letter-spacing -0.03em, cream #F0E8D2, broken to 1–2 words per line.
- Support: one DM Sans 400 lead line at 40px in 58%-opacity cream, or a cream info-card inset (no border, no shadow, square corners).
- Chrome: no shadows and no rounded corners anywhere; any page number must be a slide-internal element.

## Preview Ingredients

- Palette: bg-dark #232E26; bg-dark-alt #2E3D30; bg-cream #EDE6D0; bg-cream-alt #E4DAC4; ink-cream #F0E8D2; ink-dark #1E2820; accent-orange #C07030; wood-glow #7A4E24
- Typography: Bricolage Grotesque; DM Sans; DM Mono
- Signature move: Dark forest green canvas (#232E26) with a warm wood-brown radial glow (`atmospheric-glow`) anchored to the bottom-right corner of every dark slide.
- Signature move: Cream text (#F0E8D2) floats directly on the green field with no card, panel, or frame around it.
- Signature move: Bricolage Grotesque display type is always mixed case — never uppercase. Uppercase is reserved for DM Mono labels.
- Signature move: A single accent color, warm orange (#C07030), used only as small inline emphasis (kicker, bullet marker, inline <em>, quote glyph, one chart bar).
- Signature move: The cream info-card (`info-card`) is the signature material-contrast component — a warm paper rectangle inset on the dark field.
