## Visual Snapshot

Cover recipe:

- Surface: full-bleed warm parchment #E9E5DB inside 77px edge padding; no cards, no borders, no shadows, zero border-radius.
- Atmosphere: one large sun-bloom radial of #F1EE2E → #F8F39B → #F0DA7C fading to paper, sized ~42% × 38% of the stage and set off-center behind the title; optional small #E26B4A ember bloom at 15-22% opacity in the opposite corner.
- Eyebrow: Archivo weight 600 uppercase 25px, 0.18em tracking, ink #1B2566, sitting above a 1px solid ink hairline rule.
- Title: Instrument Serif weight 400 at 368px, line-height 0.86, letter-spacing -0.018em, ink #1B2566, left-aligned — the single display moment on the slide.
- Date rail: Instrument Serif 151px stacked at top-right, right-aligned, en-dash for spans.
- Footer band: four-column metadata strip pinned to the bottom, 46px gaps, each cell a 1px ink hairline top border + Archivo 600 uppercase 21px tag + 23px body line.
- Page number: JetBrains Mono 25px `01 / NN` at bottom-right, ink at 0.75 opacity, 26px up / 46px in — authored as a slide-internal element, never `.progress-bar` / `.slide-counter`.
