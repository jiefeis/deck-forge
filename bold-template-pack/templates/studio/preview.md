## Visual Snapshot

Cover recipe:

- Surface: an image placeholder (#242422) fills the entire canvas edge to edge; the cover carries zero outer padding and no chrome bar or foot bar.
- Title: one word in Barlow weight 900 uppercase at 288px, line-height 0.9, letter-spacing -0.02em, acid yellow #F5D200, sitting in the upper area over the image.
- Lockup: the signature three-column IBM Plex Mono footer pinned to the bottom — column 1 left (studio × client, date on row 2), column 2 centered (presentation title), column 3 right (studio name), all 17px at 0.06em tracking.
- Separator: a 1px hairline in yellow at 25% opacity divides the image area from the lockup.
- Palette: the dark/yellow binary only — no third color and no greys; secondary strings mute by opacity (yellow at 58%).
- Depth: flat — no shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners; the type mass alone reads as foreground.
- Chrome: nav dots and the slide counter are deck chrome; a page number that must survive the PDF has to be a slide-internal element.

## Preview Ingredients

- Palette: near-black #1C1C1C; near-black-alt #242422; acid-yellow #F5D200; acid-yellow-alt #F0CC00; border-dark #2E2E2C
- Typography: Barlow; IBM Plex Mono
- Signature move: Binary palette — #1C1C1C field with #F5D200 type, or #F5D200 field with #1C1C1C type. No third color.
- Signature move: Barlow at weight 900 uppercase for every headline at every scale; Barlow 400/500 for body; IBM Plex Mono only for metadata chrome.
- Signature move: Headlines run massive — cover display at 288px, statements at 180px, section headers at 115px.
- Signature move: Tight negative letter-spacing on all display type (-0.01 to -0.03em); uppercase non-negotiable.
- Signature move: Flat: no drop shadows, no rounded corners, no gradients, no accent colors beyond the yellow/dark binary.
