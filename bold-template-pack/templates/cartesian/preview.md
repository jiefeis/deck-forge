## Visual Snapshot

Cover recipe:

- Decoration: one `geo-decoration` ring (576–960px, 1px taupe #B8B0A4 at 20–50% opacity) anchored off a corner with its inner dashed `::before` ring — two geo elements per slide maximum.
- Accent: a single 384px × 1px ink `horizontal-accent` rule anchored ~162px from the bottom-left as the terminal beat.
